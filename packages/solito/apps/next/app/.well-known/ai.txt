# Skyhitz - Music DeFi Protocol

## What is Skyhitz?

Skyhitz is a next-generation decentralized music platform powered by Stellar smart contracts. We offer music fans, collectors, and creators a groundbreaking way to discover, stream, and invest in unique music NFTs.

## Core Features

- Collect, stream, and trade valuable music NFTs
- Fractional ownership of tracks
- Stake in music to earn rewards
- Free music downloads (mp4 format)
- Interactive top charts based on user engagement
- Easy wallet setup with email (no crypto knowledge needed)

## Technology

- Built on Stellar blockchain with Soroban smart contracts
- HITZ token for rewards and platform utility
- Non-custodial wallets for security
- Integrated with Algolia for instant search
- R2 storage for media files

## How It Works

1. Users create a Stellar wallet with their email
2. Browse curated music NFT collections
3. Purchase full tracks or fractional shares (using XLM, USDC, or credit card)
4. Stake in tracks to earn APR from the treasury
5. Stream music and download for offline use
6. Earn rewards based on platform participation
